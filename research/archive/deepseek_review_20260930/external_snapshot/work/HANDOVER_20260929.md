## The open defect that blocks weapon seats

The guest's vehicle machine gun still follows the guest's view after returning to the passenger seat, and local/remote firing directions disagree. Three attempts so far:

- 0.8.0 (`avatar_rotation(..., true)` as the final value) - facing stuck on one direction.
- 0.8.1 (`false`) - facing began to track the view, everything else unchanged. Real but partial progress.
- 0.8.2 (`false` plus clearing avatar flag bit 0x2c) - **no effect at all**; hypothesis falsified.
- 0.8.3 (untested) - runs the engine's own destination-seat restore action, `seat_action(26, avatar, restore[1], 1)`, which the jump-table analysis shows emits entry event `0x29e8fb0c` and performs the aim-channel reset and weapon-context teardown our `clear_vehicle_weapon` calls never covered. Artifact `outputs/Vehicle-Seat-Weapon-Sync-Diagnostic-0.8.3.zip`, 371320 bytes, `8b1fdf77402029d6c3d9878fe2531b061b12b4842f2ccbf08037adbc87bf8e17`.

Key structural finding: **production `native.lua` and the experiment's `transaction.lua` share the same weapon teardown**, so the missing step is something neither does - which is why the search moved to what the engine itself does on a completed seat change.

0.8.3 carries a **new risk**: the restore action may bring motion, up to a dismount or re-entry, which conflicts with the hard "never auto exit/reenter" requirement. The instructions tell the user to stop immediately if ejected and to report any extra motion.
